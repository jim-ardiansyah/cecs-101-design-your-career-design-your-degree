### What's a RIASEC Profile?
---
The RIASEC framework is a career and interest assessment model that helps individuals better understand their interests, preferences, and potential career pathways. Developed from the work of psychologist John Holland, the framework identifies six broad interest themes that can be used to explore how a person's interests may connect with academic programs, work environments, and career opportunities. These themes are often referred to as Holland Codes.

The six RIASEC themes are:

- **R — Realistic:** People who enjoy hands-on activities, working with tools or machines, building things, and solving practical problems.
- **I — Investigative:** People who enjoy researching, analyzing information, asking questions, solving complex problems, and understanding how things work.
- **A — Artistic:** People who enjoy creativity, self-expression, design, writing, music, visual arts, and developing new ideas.
- **S — Social:** People who enjoy helping, teaching, supporting, communicating with, and working closely with others.
- **E — Enterprising:** People who enjoy leading, persuading, organizing, managing, taking initiative, and pursuing business or entrepreneurial opportunities.
- **C — Conventional:** People who enjoy organizing information, working with data, following established processes, paying attention to details, and creating structured systems.

The six themes are commonly represented as a hexagon. Themes that are next to each other on the hexagon tend to share more characteristics, while themes farther apart tend to have fewer similarities. This arrangement can help you understand why certain activities or work environments may feel more natural or comfortable than others.

### Your RIASEC Profile
---

Most people do not fit into just one category. Instead, individuals typically have a combination of two or three dominant interest themes. For example, someone with an Investigative-Artistic-Social (IAS) profile might enjoy researching problems, developing creative solutions, and helping others understand or use those solutions.

Your RIASEC profile can provide a starting point for exploring:

- Academic majors and areas of study
- Potential career paths
- Internship and job opportunities
- Work environments that may be a good fit
- Activities and projects you may enjoy
- Skills you may want to develop

It is also important to remember that your interests are not fixed. Your RIASEC profile may change as you gain new experiences, take different courses, develop new skills, or discover new interests. For some people, their interests remain relatively consistent over time; for others, they evolve significantly.

Most importantly, you know yourself better than any assessment does. A RIASEC assessment is a tool for reflection and exploration—not a test that determines what career you should pursue. Use the results as a starting point to think about what you enjoy, what motivates you, and what types of environments allow you to do your best work. There are no "right" or "wrong" RIASEC results. The purpose is to help you become more intentional about designing your degree and exploring a career path that fits who you are.

### Interests Aren't Fixed — and That's Okay
---

One important caveat: your RIASEC profile is not a permanent identity. For some people, interests stay remarkably stable from adolescence into adulthood. For others, interests shift meaningfully as they gain new experiences, take on new responsibilities, or simply grow into different versions of themselves. Neither pattern is more "correct" than the other.

What matters most is that no framework, assessment, or code can know your interests better than you do. RIASEC is a tool for organizing self-reflection — a starting point for conversation, not a verdict. Use it to notice patterns, name what draws you in, and open up possibilities you might not have considered. But the final call on what excites you, what drains you, and what you want to build a career around will always be yours to make.
