# Design Your Degree, Design Your Career

Welcome to **Design Your Degree, Design Your Career**!

This course provides students with the opportunity to take an active role in shaping their academic and professional journeys within the **College of Emerging and Collaborative Studies (CECS)**. Using principles of design thinking, you will explore your interests, strengths, values, and goals to create a customized educational pathway aligned with your future aspirations.

Through interactive activities, reflective exercises, and project-based learning, you will examine the connections between academic disciplines, career opportunities, and real-world challenges. The course emphasizes **creativity, adaptability, intentional planning, and lifelong learning** to help you prepare for an evolving professional landscape.

By the end of the course, you will have developed a personalized degree and career framework that reflects your individual aspirations and provides a foundation for pursuing meaningful, purpose-driven work.

## Why Study at CECS?

The **College of Emerging and Collaborative Studies at the University of Tennessee, Knoxville** empowers students to create their own academic and professional pathways in innovative and rapidly evolving fields such as **artificial intelligence, data science, and cybersecurity**.

With an emphasis on hands-on learning, interdisciplinary collaboration, and industry partnerships, CECS prepares students for the careers of tomorrow by combining technical knowledge with practical, collaborative experience.
