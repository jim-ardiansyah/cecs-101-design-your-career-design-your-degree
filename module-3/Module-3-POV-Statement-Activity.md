# POV Statement Activity

Your POV Statement is the foundation of your entire Define Phase. It crystallizes your self-knowledge into a single, honest, actionable articulation of your academic direction. This is not a formal mission statement — it is a working tool. It will evolve. But you need a starting point, and this activity helps you build one.

---

- Your SWOT Matrix (especially Strengths and Opportunities)
- Empathy Map

## Step 3 — Draft Your POV Statement

Use the following template to draft your POV Statement. Write it in your own voice — not in formal or academic language. Write **at least two drafts** of your POV Statement and choose the one that feels most honest and most useful.

> "I am [brief description of who you are — background, identity, situation]. I need [a genuine academic or career need you identified]. Because [the most important insight from your self-assessment work]."

**Example:**
> "I am a first-generation college student from a small town who has always been drawn to science but also deeply values connection to people and community. I need a degree path that lets me combine analytical thinking with direct human impact, because I have learned that I lose motivation and meaning when my work feels disconnected from real people's lives."
