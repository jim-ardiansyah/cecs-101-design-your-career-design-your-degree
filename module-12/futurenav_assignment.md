# Navigate Your Future: FutureNav and CliftonStrengths Exploration

## Purpose

Your career journey starts before graduation. Some of the best career decisions are made by students who take time early to understand their strengths, interests, and experiences, and then connect them to real opportunities. In this assignment you'll use **FutureNav**, a career exploration platform, along with the **CliftonStrengths** assessment to see how who you are connects to where you could go.

This is not a final career decision. It's an exercise in self-discovery, exploring possibilities, and taking a first step toward planning your degree and career pathway.

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Take the CliftonStrengths Assessment

The CliftonStrengths assessment is part of FutureNav, so complete it first.

1. Go to the UTK Strengths for Students page: [https://strengths.utk.edu/strengths-for-students/](https://strengths.utk.edu/strengths-for-students/)
2. Follow the instructions there to take the assessment
3. Set aside enough uninterrupted time to finish it in one sitting, and go with your first instinct on each question
4. When you finish, save or download your results. You'll refer to them in the next steps

### Step 2: Access FutureNav

Explore the UTK section of the FutureNav Compass platform:

[https://compass.futurenav.ets.org/utk](https://compass.futurenav.ets.org/utk)

1. Sign in or create your account as prompted
2. Complete your profile and the activities FutureNav gives you
3. Take your time exploring. The more you engage with the platform, the more useful your results will be

### Step 3: Explore and Connect

Look at how the information you discover connects to you. As you explore, consider:

**A. Strengths:** How do your CliftonStrengths results fit the careers FutureNav suggests?

**B. Skills and experiences:** Which of your classes, jobs, activities, or volunteer roles connect to these careers?

**C. Interests:** Which careers or fields stand out to you, and why?

**D. Opportunities:** What majors, internships, or experiences could help you prepare for these paths?

### Step 4: Plan Your Next Step

Use what you found to begin designing your degree and career pathway:

- Identify **two or three** careers or pathways that interest you
- Choose **one action** you can take this semester, such as meeting with an advisor, joining an organization, or looking for an internship
- Think about how this information might shape your degree plan
